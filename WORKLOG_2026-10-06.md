Phase 3a (first agent round, kept in benchmark_results/runs_v1_agents/; 17 of 20 finished before I

NUMERICAL_FAILURE at 107.6 min after repeated throughput cuts. Diagnosis from the decision logs:
1. NO_ACTION was accepted without a twin rollout. After the twin rejected a real fix, the rule agent
   fell back to NO_ACTION and validation passed it (S09, C03, D01, D03, T01: the agents watched the plant
   trip).
2. A latched non-trip SIF (e.g. R_P_HH after the H2 PCV degrades) never cleared because nobody requested

3. Several failed pumps were tried one at a time; each alone still left the other pump failed (T01).

6. The twin was a deep copy that also contained the *future* fault-lift times (it "knew" when faults
   would clear) and future injections.

Fixes (agents/recovery_graph.py, agents/supervisor.py, agents/action.py):
- NO_ACTION is rolled out by the twin like any plan; accepted only if the plant recovers by itself.
- Counterfactual fallback: if nothing restores the plant, the "do nothing" rollout is the baseline and the
  candidate that does best against it is applied as a mitigation. Ranking (`consequence_key`): no trip
  (or a later one) > more faulty targets back within limits > less time unsafe > lower PCA T²+SPE.
  Previously only candidates that were fully "safe" could be applied.
- `AgentConfig.sif_reset=True`: the agents request a SIF reset while non-trip SIFs are latched (README
  lists SIF reset as an operator/agent action). The SIF logic only resets a latch whose variable is back
  in its reset band; plant-trip latches are left for the operator. The twin does the same.
- Twin knows only the present (`clone_env(present_only=True)`): active faults persist, future ones removed.
- `mitigation_wait_min=10`: after a mitigation the agents re-decide after 10 min (was 30), so a second
  or third fault can be handled.
- Rule agent: a combined "start every standby" candidate; drum-draining candidate raises the TOP_TIC
  setpoint +3 / +6 K (less reflux), combined with spares if any; rule_twin reprompt_max 2 -> 4.
- Test updated: the counterfactual rollout is excluded when counting rollouts per proposal.
