      Protocol v1 vs v2

      v1 re-decides every 5 minutes while a fault is detected; v2 decides once per fault episode (alarm or SPE trigger; a new safety alarm or a newly failed pump opens a new decision), with plain-language feedback and the ADMIT record re-check. Same 20 scenarios and economics.

      LLMs compared

      The same 20 scenarios, fault-lift times and economics for every LLM; only the model behind the agents changes. Open models ran locally through Ollama (4-bit quantised, 16k context, temperature 0) on one 8 GB laptop GPU.

        Controller setpoint tests

        Each PI loop from the nominal steady state, sensor noise on: setpoint step up at 5 min, back to the design value at 45 min, run to 85 min. Pass = output moves the right way and PV ends within 20 % of the step (or two deadbands) after both steps, no SIF, no priority-1 alarm.
